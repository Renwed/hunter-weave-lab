What just happened

    ? What’s the dead zone?Why you walk in to 5 yd and back out past 11.67 yd

    The dead zone is the gap where you can’t do anything to the target: you are too far away to melee but too close to shoot. A bow, gun or crossbow won’t fire inside its minimum range, so Auto Shot, Aimed Shot, Arcane Shot, Serpent Sting and Multi-Shot all stop working there.

    Where 11.67 yd comes from

    The game measures both ranges from the edge of each unit’s combat reach (its body size), not from its centre:

    1. Melee rangeyour reach + boss reach + 1⅓ yd (never less than 5 yd)
    2. Shot minimumyour reach + boss reach + 8 yd
    3. Dead zone width8 − 1⅓ = 6⅔ yd

    Both reaches cancel out, so the gap is 6⅔ yd however big the boss is. WoWSims puts the melee edge at 5 yd, so shooting starts at 5 + 8 − 1⅓ = 11.67 yd. A bigger boss moves both edges further out, but the gap stays the same width.

    This is how the WoWSims Forever code defines it (MinRangedRange). In game, against a small, player-sized target, the gap measures closer to 6 yd.

    What it costs you to weave

    • 7.07 ydper crossing (11.67 → 4.60)
    • 1.01 sat normal run speed (7 yd/s)
    • 0.78 swith Strider Kick’s +30% speed (9.1 yd/s)
    • 46%of this fight spent walking: 125 crossings, 116 of 253 s

    A weave walks the zone twice, which takes about 1.8 to 2 s. You can’t shoot while moving, so the APL only walks in right after an Auto Shot. It also checks that you can swing and get back out before the next shot is due. It will accept delaying that shot by up to about 3 s (the “Max Auto Shot delay” setting). The payoff: Raptor Strike, Strider Kick, Mongoose Bite and white swings did 29% of your damage in this run.

    Tips in game

    • Walk in right after an Auto Shot fires, so the swing and the walk back fit inside the time before the next shot.
    • Stop a little inside melee range. The APL aims for 4.60 yd, not 5, so small movements don’t knock you out of range mid-swing.
    • Use Strider Kick before you walk out. Its 3-second speed boost cuts the trip back from about 1.01 s to 0.78 s.
    • Use your action bar instead of counting yards. Your shot buttons turn red inside the dead zone. Step back until they light up again and you are right at the edge.
